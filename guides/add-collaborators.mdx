Nyckel lets you invite other people to work on the functions in your account. This is useful when multiple team members need to annotate data, review function performance, or build integrations using the API.

## What collaborators can and can't do

Collaborators you add to your account can:

- Create, view, edit, and delete functions in your account
- Invoke functions using their own credentials

Collaborators **cannot**:

- View or edit your billing details

<Note>
  A collaborator must already have a Nyckel account before you can add them. If they don't have one yet, ask them to sign up at [nyckel.com/console](https://www.nyckel.com/console) first.
</Note>

## Billing

All invocations made against functions in your account are billed to your account — regardless of which credentials were used. This applies to invocations made by collaborators using their own credentials just as it does to your own.

## How to add a collaborator

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open account settings">
    Go to the [Nyckel Console](https://www.nyckel.com/console) and navigate to your account settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite by email">
    Enter the email address associated with the collaborator's Nyckel account and send the invitation. Once accepted, they will have access to all functions in your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>
